SmartTO

Who we are

Smart Mobility Applied Research and Testing-Technologies of Ontario (SmartTO)-YSpace is part of the York Region Regional Technology Development Site (RTDS); a part of the Ontario Vehicle Innovation Network (OVIN). We are a transportation and mobility hub led by York University in partnership with Centennial College. We empower startups and SMEs to develop, test and prototype advanced automotive technologies and smart mobility solutions through accessing our state of the-art equipment, innovative labs and research centres, world-renowned expertise, and concierge-style business advisory and technical services. We focus on Smart Materials and Light-weighting along with Smart Mobility, Connectivity and Reliability.

What we offer

State-of-the-art R&D Facilities 

R&D space with a full suite of technologies, test vehicles, specialized equipment, and technical support including access to Canada's largest on-site transportation training facility.

Feasibility Studies/Optimal Design & Software

Access our experts and resources to test and develop your ideas with the latest tools and software on the market - from battery testing to software design.

Living and Virtual Lab Testing Environment

Test your technology in real-world conditions by using York University’s campus, Canada’s 3rd largest university and a WWF-Canada recognized Living Campus, as a Living and Virtual Lab.

Workshops & Training 

Industry specific workshops and on-demand training on topics like Technology Commercialization and Technology Entrepreneurship.

Concierge-Style Entrepreneurial Support

Customized business advisory services with top industry experts and peer-to-peer networking support

Technology Accelerator & Incubator Programs

Learn how validate and become market-ready and, if you're ready, develop and execute your go-to-market strategy.

SmartTO unites two institutional powerhouses. Together, we are the hub for innovation in the automotive technology and smart mobility sector.

SmartTO clients get access to the largest transportation training facility in Canada at Centennial College with exclusive use of test vehicles and specialized equipment for prototyping and validation. Our world-class research capability includes utilizing York University Keele Campus as a Living Lab, allowing testing of innovative technology in a real-life context. Clients also have access to York University's Campus Digital Twin, a complete digital replica of the university's Keele Campus that simulates pedestrian and vehicle mobility to test smart mobility solutions.

Ontario Vehicle Innovation Network

SmartTO part of the York Region,  regional technology development site supported by the Ontario Vehicle Innovation Network (OVIN). A key component of the Government of Ontario’s plan for positioning Ontario as a North American leader in developing emerging technologies and advanced manufacturing processes, OVIN harnesses Ontario’s regional strengths and capabilities and bridges its automotive and technology clusters to promote the development of Ontario-made electric vehicles, advanced automotive technologies, and smart mobility solutions.
