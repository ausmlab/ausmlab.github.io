Dr. Seungho Yang will give a presentation at the 54th Annual Canadian Transportation Research Forum, which will happen on May 26-29, 2019 in Vancouver, Canada. Dr. Yang will give his presentation on “Public Transit Service Reliability Assessment Using Two-Fluid Model Based on Big GPS Data” (Seungo Yang, Afnan Ahmad, Peter Park and Gunho Sohn). This presentation is a research collaboration outcome between Transportation (Dr. Peter Park) and Geomatics Engineering (Dr. Gunho Sohn and Afnan Ahmad). This research work was supported by GO Transit (Jeremy Krygsman).
