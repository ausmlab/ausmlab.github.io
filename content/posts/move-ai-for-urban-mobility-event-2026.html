On June 22, 2026, the Mobility Innovation Centre (MOVE), directed by Prof. Gunho Sohn, hosted “AI for Urban Mobility: Connecting Data, Research & Industry.”

The event brought together York University researchers and industry partners to talk about how data and AI can improve how people and goods move in cities. MOVE members Prof. Mehdi Nourinejad, Prof. Manos Papagelis, Prof. Shweta Mahajan, Prof. James Elder and Prof. Hina Tabassum presented their work.
