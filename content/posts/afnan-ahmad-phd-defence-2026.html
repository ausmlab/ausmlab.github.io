Congratulations to Dr. Afnan Ahmad, who successfully defended his Ph.D. dissertation in Earth and Space Science at York University on August 20, 2026. The oral examination was held online, with Prof. Bilal Farooq (Toronto Metropolitan University) as external examiner.

His dissertation, “Online Adaptive Deep Learning for Multi-Sensor WiFi Indoor Localization,” tackles a long-standing problem in indoor positioning: Wi-Fi signals change over time, so a model trained one year slowly loses accuracy the next. Afnan’s work makes two main contributions:

  • Label-free online adaptation. A deep model fuses Wi-Fi signal strength with phone inertial and magnetometer data, and adjusts its features while it is being used, without new labelled positions. On the York University testbed it reaches a mean error of about half a metre.
  • A multi-year indoor benchmark. Data from four York University buildings collected in 2021 and 2022, with HoloLens 2 augmented-reality ground truth, to test how well models hold up across years.

Afnan joined the lab in 2017 for his master’s degree and continued into the Ph.D. program. Congratulations, Dr. Ahmad!
