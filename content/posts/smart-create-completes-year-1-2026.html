The NSERC CREATE program Smart Mobility Advanced Research & Training (SMART) has completed its first year. The program is led by Principal Investigator Prof. Gunho Sohn.

In its first year, SMART has grown into a national training network for future mobility research. It now brings together ten co-investigators, fifteen trainees, and more than twenty partners from universities, industry and government.
