On September 24, 2026, Prof. Gunho Sohn joined the panel at Industry Night: Computing & Mechanics 2026, a student-run networking event at York University organized by the Industry Night Initiative with the IEEE York University Student Branch and the York University Robotics Society (YURS). The event ran from 3:30 to 8:30 pm.

He shared the stage with Peter Koster (Qualcomm), Ninad Joshi (Pratt & Whitney), Emile Lecoultre (QNX) and Prof. Sunila Akbar. Companies taking part included QNX, AMD, OPG, Qualcomm, SEW-Eurodrive, Honda, Mecsmart and Pratt & Whitney.
