Congratulations to Mohammadjavad Ghorbanalivakili, who successfully defended his Ph.D. thesis on AI perception for autonomous trains.

His defence covered TRIT-Net, a network that traces the train's ego-path track by track, and a railway-adapted vision-language model that reasons about the scene before segmenting the path ahead (ISPRS 2026).

Mohammadjavad continues in the lab as a postdoctoral fellow from October 2026, working on railway perception and sidewalk delivery robots.
