Ms. Mercedes Boateng joined our lab from September 10th, 2020 as a project coordinator. Mercedes will contribute to our ongoing research projects as the project coordinator, particularly for 3D Mobile Mapping Artificial Intelligence (3DMMAI) project and Intelligent Systems for Sustainable Urban Mobility (ISSUM). Mercedes graduate from the Psychology Department at York University and has been working as an office assistant at York University. Our lab is very excited about having Mercedes who will manage the projects and lab’s administration. Welcome, Mercedes to our lab!
