MOVE and CRESS at York University have jointly won a research contract with the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (KASI).

The project is part of Korea’s Space Situational Awareness (K-SSA) Program. York will develop AI that detects and tracks resident space objects — satellites and debris — in images from KASI’s SpaceOWL satellite.

The project runs to October 2030. Prof. Gunho Sohn is Principal Investigator. Prof. Regina Lee is Co-Principal Investigator.

The project builds on more than 15 years of collaboration under an MOU between York University and KASI.
