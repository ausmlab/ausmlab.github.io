Prof. Gunho Sohn and Prof. Regina Lee took part in the Korea–Canada Transpacific Relations Forum in Toronto.

Prof. Sohn sat on a panel with speakers from industry, finance and the Consulate General of the Republic of Korea. The panel discussed how Korea and Canada can build stronger ties through mutual confidence and knowledge-led innovation.
