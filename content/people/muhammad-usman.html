Muhammad Usman is a Postdoctoral Fellow with the Ontario Research Fund – Intelligent Systems for Sustainable Urban Mobility (ORF–ISSUM) at York University. He received his M.Sc. (2016) and Ph.D. (2020) degree in Computer Science from York University. His research interests include crowd simulation, human-building interaction, virtual environments, crowd behaviours in immersive VR, computer-aided architectural design analysis and optimization, and spatial visualizations.
